Alison's Skor Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chef: Alison

Brown butter chocolate chip cookies with crunchy Skor toffee pieces.

Instructions:

  1. Brown the butter over medium heat, stirring for 5–8 minutes. Cool for 10 minutes.
  2. Combine the flour, baking soda, and kosher salt in a separate bowl.
  3. Beat both sugars into the butter, then beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mix in the dry ingredients. Fold in the Skor and chocolate.
  5. Rest the dough for 30 minutes.
  6. Heat the oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  7. Scoop dough portions 3 inches apart. Sprinkle with flaky salt.
  8. Bake for 9–11 minutes. Cool on the sheet for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

Tips:

  • Add chopped walnuts for extra crunch.
  • Store cooled cookies in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
  • Freeze extra dough in individual portions to bake later.
  • Let the browned butter cool before adding the eggs.
  • Use a cool baking sheet for each batch.
